# 🏨 Hotel Reservation Management System

## 📌 Overview

The **Hotel Reservation Management System** is a Java-based application developed as part of a **second-year, first-semester group project**.

The system streamlines hotel operations by managing:

- Reservations  
- Administrative operations  
- Customer service processes  
- Financial records  

It provides an organized and efficient platform for handling hotel bookings and internal management tasks.

---

## ✨ Core Features

### 👨‍💼 Admin Module
- System-wide management and control  
- Manage operational records  
- Monitor overall system activities  

---

### 🛎️ Reservation Management
- Create new reservations  
- Update existing bookings  
- Cancel reservations  
- Track room availability  
- Handle booking confirmations  
- Maintain structured reservation records  

---

### 📞 Customer Service Management
- Manage customer inquiries  
- Handle service requests  
- Provide support during booking and stay  

---

### 💰 Finance Management
- Track payments and transactions  
- Maintain financial records  
- Support revenue monitoring
